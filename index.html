Contact the developer

Found a problem or have a question? Send an email with the details below.

astratacticssupport@gmail.com
Email support ↗

What to include

  1. 01Device model and iOS / iPadOS version
  2. 02App version, and where the problem occurred
  3. 03Steps to reproduce the problem
  4. 04A screenshot, if it helps — remove personal information first

Before you write

A purchase or billing issue?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s purchase support page.

Apple purchase support

A problem with progress or settings?

Describe what you were doing and the last working state. Save data and settings are stored on your device. Please do not delete the app while troubleshooting: local data may be lost.

Do I need an account?

No account or login is required. The game is a paid app without advertising.
