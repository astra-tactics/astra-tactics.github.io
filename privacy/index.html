Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in ASTRA TACTICS and on this support website. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to the developer at the email below.

1. In the game

ASTRA TACTICS does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect gameplay activity, device identifiers or location through the game, and does not run a server for player profiles.

2. Progress and settings

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. App Store purchases and payment information are handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your card details.

3. When you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and resolve the reported problem, not for advertising. Email is handled through Gmail under Google’s policies. Avoid sending sensitive information.

4. Retention and your choices

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, follow up on the issue or satisfy applicable legal obligations. You can ask to access, correct or delete information you sent by contacting the email below. Local game data can be removed through your device’s app data controls or by deleting the app.

5. This website and external services

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The developer does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Opening email or an external link involves the policies of the selected email service, GitHub or Apple.

6. Children and updates

The game does not request personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can request deletion. This policy will be updated when information-handling practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
